# On Notes

A personal library should make reading effortless.

When notes live as plain files, they stay portable. When the app turns those files into articles, they become pleasant to revisit.

## What matters

1. **Title and summary** that help you find the right document quickly
2. **Tags** that describe subject, project, or status
3. **Native rendering** instead of a white PDF page

> Keep the source files yours. Index them; do not trap them.
